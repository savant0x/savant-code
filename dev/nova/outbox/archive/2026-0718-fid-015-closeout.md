## 4 Caller Updates Required

**Zero caller updates required.** All SDK callers (`change-file.ts`, `apply-patch.ts`) use `resolvedPath.fullPath` directly for `fs.writeFile`. POSIX paths work natively on both platforms:
- Linux: `fs.writeFile('/repo/src/file.ts')` writes to `/repo/src/file.ts`
- Windows: `fs.writeFile('/repo/src/file.ts')` writes to current-drive-root + `/repo/src/file.ts` (typically `C:\repo\src\file.ts`)
