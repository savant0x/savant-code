# Ambition in Motion

## Learning objectives

- Accept that a clear top-level vision and an unclear first step can coexist.
- Avoid getting stuck in grand narratives by taking imperfect actions that generate new data.
- Use contextual coaching instead of relying only on abstract advice.

## Core ideas

Altman compares effective startup coaching to flight instruction: observe the learner in action, point out missed signals, correct mistakes in context, and direct undirected energy toward something that can move forward. `claim-ch3-coaching`

For ambitious projects, he argues that founders do not need a complete roadmap before starting, but they must take steps that generate new data. A grand vision that makes no progress for a long time is a different failure mode from a vision that is itself wrong. The source gives no universal cadence or quantitative checkpoint, so applications should define observable feedback with the user. `claim-ch3-motion`

## Evidence modality

Both methods are treated only as explanations in the speaker's words. This chapter does not claim that the video contains a visually verifiable demonstration.

## Common failure modes

- Using the scale of the vision to excuse a long period without progress.
- Choosing busywork that produces no new information.
- Giving large amounts of generic advice without observing the project in context.
- Imposing the same iteration cadence on every project.

## Knowledge check

Distinguish among a wrong vision, a wrong first step, and stalled execution. What different evidence does each diagnosis require?

## Micro-exercise

Describe one step that may not succeed but will produce observable data by the next review. Do not impose a fixed number of days that the source never specifies.
