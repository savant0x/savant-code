# Founder Thesis Stress Test

> This is a generator-created exercise, not an assignment from the original video. Do not load or reveal the rubric under `solutions/` when first presenting the exercise.

## Task

Choose a real or hypothetical AI startup and submit a thesis of no more than 900 words that covers:

1. **Shift conditions:** What change in technology, cost, cycle time, or incumbent advantage makes this possible now?
2. **Higher ceiling:** What previously unreasonable problem can you take on because of AI leverage?
3. **Business fundamentals that still apply:** Cover at least three of user value, taste, agency, business physics, and moat.
4. **Non-consensus hypothesis:** What do other people not believe? State the evidence that would strengthen and weaken the hypothesis.
5. **People and networks:** Which small group of shared believers matters most? What non-transactional help can you offer first?
6. **Next step:** Name one action that will generate new data even if it fails, together with its observable outcome.
7. **Dual constraint:** State the minimum safety requirement, concentration-of-power risk, and a checkpoint for user freedom or agency.

## Success criteria

- Clearly distinguish video evidence, facts about your project, and generator inference.
- Do not use the video's inconsistent exact productivity figures as benchmarks.
- Include at least one piece of disconfirming evidence, not only supporting material.
- Make the next step generate new information instead of merely producing activity.
- Examine both safety and concentration of power without pretending that the video supplies concrete thresholds.

## Submission process

Submit the complete thesis first. The coach should then load only the rubric, identify the smallest repairable gap, and allow one retry. Show the example answer only after the learner has attempted the exercise or explicitly asks for it.
