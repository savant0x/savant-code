# Free-options filtering

Discovery collects broad evidence. Boot and `/provider free` derive a coding
selection from that evidence; the artifact at the displayed `free-options.md`
path retains every catalog model and offer for inspection. Fetching and storage
do not silently discard unrelated categories.

The coding selection excludes duplicate provider/model pairs, expired models,
unreadable stated expirations, specialist media/retrieval/safety tasks and models
explicitly reported without text output or tool support. Provider aliases share
one identity. Vision/omni names without metadata remain unverified and are kept
in the broad report; explicit text-output metadata can admit a multimodal chat
model. The older strict policy for other provider catalogs remains in place.

The catalog lift preserves output-modalities and supported-parameters arrays
when supplied, including through cache round trips. A name-based candidate is
not a capability certificate. Counts and copy explicitly identify candidates
without text/tool metadata; no inference or account-quota probe runs here.

Offers in Coding or API providers are considered relevant unless their title
identifies a specialist task. Other categories, unsafe/malformed links,
duplicates, expired offers and unresolved advertised deadlines remain in the
broad discovery report. Relevance does not establish eligibility: signup,
subscription, student or regional conditions still require checking the offer.
The system does not claim these credits are available to the current account.

The selector receives its clock explicitly. Date-only deadlines last through
the stated UTC day; timestamp deadlines use their stated instant. Ending-soon
counts and new-model notices are derived from the selected rows, not raw totals.

The panel remains collapsed by default. Expanded output and Copy share the same
preview: at most five model IDs per provider and five offers across categories.
Header counts describe all selected rows before truncation, and the remainder
line points to the complete discovery artifact. Preview order follows source
order; it is not a quality ranking or a recommendation based on benchmarks.

Known limit: the current aggregated catalog often lacks capability metadata.
The conservative name classifier removes recognizable specialist tasks but
cannot prove an opaque model's purpose, real tool behavior or account access.
Such rows remain candidates with an explicit uncertainty label. Public offers
are discovery evidence, not verified vendor pricing or quota guarantees.
