Architectural Research Study: Savant-Native Capability Extraction from dsh-superagent and Elephant-Goldfish Model1. Executive conclusionAn architectural evaluation of the Savant-Code harness alongside the DeepSeek dsh-superagent research prototype and Google's elephant-goldfish-model (EGM) reveals distinct paradigms in autonomous agent engineering. Savant-Code operates as a strictly governed, single-committer harness enforcing mathematical correctness, zero-trust provenance, and deterministic state transitions via the ECHO Protocol v0.2.0. In contrast, dsh-superagent is an asynchronous, event-driven supervisor designed to minimize human attention overhead across continuous GitHub repository operations. The elephant-goldfish-model is an artifact-first review harness focused on eliminating conversational blind spots through radical context asymmetry between authoring and reviewing agents.The investigation identifies five foundational capabilities that offer significant architectural upgrades for Savant-Code:Mandate-bound scope provenance and dynamic policy revalidation, adapting the dsh-superagent admission model to formalize why a task was initiated and dynamically re-verify its legitimacy before state mutations.Bounded fresh-context audit isolation, formalizing EGM's "Goldfish" isolation into Savant's zero-tool Verifier and Adversary passes with cryptographic evidence boundaries.Single-seam durable effect outbox with idempotent coalescing, integrating the dsh-superagent SQLite effect queue into Savant’s packages/database commit-journal to guarantee fault-tolerant external dispatch.Semantic two-stage Feature Implementation Document (FID) convergence, incorporating EGM’s separation of architectural validity from concrete implementation readiness into the Perfection Loop.Bounded background job and subagent lifecycle accounting, adopting dsh-superagent worker-draining semantics to enforce a strict runtime block against premature task completion claims.From dsh-superagent, the strongest mechanisms are its transactional engine discipline, mandate-driven authorization provenance, and durable side-effect queues with key-coalescing and retry backoff. From EGM, the strongest mechanisms are context-isolated adversarial review lenses, explicit reviewer completion tracking, finding resolution taxonomy, and code-grounded design drift reporting.Multiple external capabilities are already solved natively in Savant-Code and must not be imported. Savant's 10-role canonical separation of duties, sequential-thinking engine, mutation lines, Zero-Trust Agentic Provenance (ZTAP), and ECHO Harness Enforcement Layer (EHEL) write gates provide stronger guarantees than the external prototypes.External concepts that must be rejected outright include prompt-text security boundaries, uncontained local command wrappers posing as sandboxes, unbounded subagent recursion, agent self-attribution, and non-canonical scope trimming.The overarching architectural risk of excessive or uncalibrated extraction is subsystem duplication. Introducing parallel execution engines, redundant review rosters, or alternate event queues alongside Savant’s runCommit() seam, EHEL gates, and canonical ECHO roster would fracture the single source of truth, introduce race conditions, and violate Law 13 (Utility-First, Universal Logic).2.
